# SCRIPT: The Signalman Who Was a Baboon (approved by CB 2026-11-10)

Narrator: Fish Audio "Energetic Storyteller". One line per row; quoted lines are spoken by a character on screen.

 1. A signalman ran a busy railway junction for nine years, and he never said a single word.
 2. His owner lost both his legs under a train, so he can't reach his own levers anymore.
 3. So he buys a baboon at the market, and he calls him Jack.
 4. Every morning Jack pushes him to work on a little trolley along the rails,
 5. and by the end of the month, Jack is the one pulling the levers.
 6. A train toots its whistle, and Jack grabs the right key and hands it up to the driver.
 7. Then one day a lady looks out of her train window and sees a baboon running the railway,
 8. and she screams so loud that the whole carriage writes to the railway boss.
 9. So an inspector shows up, looks at the baboon, looks at the signalman, and goes,
10. "You've got a monkey running my trains?"
11. The signalman just smiles and says, "Send one."
12. So the inspector sends train after train, and he even mixes up the whistles to trick him.
13. Two toots, Jack throws the lever, four toots, the right key, he doesn't even look up,
14. and by the last train the inspector is the one checking his notes.
15. You see, every driver whistled a different number of times for every track,
16. kind of like how you know your mom's knock from the mailman's,
17. and Jack had learned every single one just by listening.
18. So the inspector goes back to his office and writes the strangest letter the railway ever sent.
19. Jack is hired, twenty cents a day and half a bottle of beer every week,
20. and in nine years on the job, he never made a single mistake.
